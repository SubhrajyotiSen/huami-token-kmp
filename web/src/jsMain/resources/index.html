Why does this page exist?

Newer Amazfit, Zepp and Xiaomi watches and bands refuse to pair with open-source companions like Gadgetbridge unless you also provide the watch's unique Bluetooth pairing key.

That key lives on Huami/Xiaomi servers next to your paired devices. This tool logs in with your account, lists the bound devices and shows each Key so you can paste it into Gadgetbridge.

  1. Pick Amazfit (Zepp app) or Xiaomi (Mi Fitness).
  2. Enter the e-mail and password of that account.
  3. Press Get Bluetooth keys and copy the key.

Your password is sent only to the vendor's login servers, never stored.

Advanced: CORS proxy prefix

Browsers block cross-origin calls. If the request is blocked, run a CORS proxy and paste its prefix here (it is prepended to every URL).
