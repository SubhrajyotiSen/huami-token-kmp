huami-token

Bluetooth Key Extractor
Why this app?

Amazfit, Zepp and Xiaomi watches need their unique Bluetooth pairing key to work with Gadgetbridge. Log in to retrieve the keys stored on your account.

1.Select your account service (Amazfit/Zepp or Xiaomi).
2.Enter your account credentials and tap 'Get Bluetooth keys'.
3.Copy the 32-character Auth Key into Gadgetbridge when pairing.
Original project & protocol reverse engineering: https://codeberg.org/argrento/huami-token
